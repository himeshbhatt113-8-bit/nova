MISSION 1: LUNAR ASCENT

Launch from Earth with twin side boosters, circle the planet once, leave orbit on a curved transfer arc to the Moon, fly three lunar orbits, then land on the Moon. Hold Space (or the big button) to fire the engines and release it to cut thrust. Nothing is automatic: you must burn for the trans-lunar injection and the retro burn yourself - with no burn the ship stays bound to Earth (or misses the Moon). On final descent keep holding to brake, deploy the gear with G, and touch down slower than 4 m/s. Turn camera follow off for a free cam.
